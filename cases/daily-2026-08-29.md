## 5. IPA-controlled multilingual accent test

- **Available output metadata:** video montage and individual-clip duration were not reliably exposed; the author describes the tests as low-resolution 0.1-megapixel generations.
- **Model evidence:** the post title explicitly identifies MiniMax H3, the attached Reddit video demonstrates the outputs, and the author publishes the complete H3 prompt plus the exact French, Spanish, and German IPA substitutions.
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Use one referenced medium-close-up of a masked woman reclining on a white couch in a dim room, with a static frame, subtle handheld micro-motion, a small head tilt, and quiet room/fabric sound. The dialogue template is “The young woman with a clear XXXXX accent (S1) says: <d>[English] YYYYY</d>”; replace XXXXX with the accent and YYYYY with an IPA transcription of the same English test sentence. Keep non-diegetic music off. The author supplies full IPA strings for French, Spanish, and German and explains an LLM-assisted method for building new phonology cheat sheets.
- **Reusable pattern:** separate semantic language from phonetic realization: keep the dialogue language tag as English while substituting the spoken line with IPA and naming the target accent outside the dialogue tag. Treat this as experimental control rather than a guaranteed recipe—the author reports caricature-like, stilted results, mispronounced clusters, poor Japanese behavior, and character-appearance bias.
