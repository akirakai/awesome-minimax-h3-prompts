## 1. Maru999 — four repaired keyframes reconstruct a source clip without mid-shot drift

- **Author:** Maru999 (@maru_999_)
- **Published:** 2026-11-19 19:00 JST
- **Video/source:** [original X post and video](https://x.com/maru_999_/status/2123355602520854741) · [direct X video view](https://x.com/maru_999_/status/2123355602520854741/video/1) · [public MP4 stream](https://ton-0.twimg.com/amplify_video/2122673808880652288/vid/avc1/1080x1920/ZEQ17rlHkYMWVmRy.mp4?tag=29)
- **Format:** public MP4 stream 1080×1920, 9:16; duration was not disclosed in the accessible source
- **Theme:** reproduce an existing anime clip after repairing inconsistent character frames with an image model
- **Prompt, accurately summarized:** explicitly forbid transparent bodies, character overlap and hiding identity under light effects. Supply four corrected stills at the same points where they occur in the source video, rather than only a first and final frame. Describe camera movement second by second. For every mouth-moving section, also provide the voice reference and its timing. The author reports that using only the first and last corrected images allowed the motion to wander in the middle, while omitting voice timing caused lip mismatch.
- **Reusable point:** when a source clip contains identity failures inside a shot, repair and pin intermediate states at their original timestamps; combine that state ledger with second-by-second camera direction and time-aligned voice, rather than asking first/last frames to carry the entire performance.
- **Model evidence:** the original post explicitly says the corrected frames were animated with “MiniMax H3,” tags both #MiniMax and #H3, publishes the video, and says the complete prompt is in the reply thread.

## 2. Sileent AI Production Studio — local H3 base generation refined into the 3:16 short “SUNSET”

- **Author:** Sileent AI Production Studio (@Sileent_AI); created and directed by Emiliano Colantoni
- **Published:** 2026-11-20 01:00 JST
- **Video/source/workflow:** [full 3:16 episode on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L9e47j2Hr84) · [original X workflow post and teaser](https://x.com/Sileent_AI/status/2123446208723144894) · [direct X video view](https://x.com/Sileent_AI/status/2123446208723144894/video/1)
- **Format:** full episode 3 minutes 16 seconds, 16:9; public X teaser stream 1920×1080; H3 generated 1K eight-second shots that were refined and upscaled to 2K
- **Theme:** inside a gigantic, human-empty data center, a malfunction wakes an autonomous robot that travels through endless corridors and technology towers, framing the ecological footprint of technology and the slow decline of humanity
- **Prompt/workflow, accurately summarized:** choose between two H3 inputs per shot: Base Reference when creative freedom is useful, and Start Frame when composition and lighting must match a predefined design. Establish each shot’s upstream geometry through a small Blender-to-ComfyUI plugin and a dedicated Krea 2 structure workflow. Generate the base video in MiniMax H3 at 1K, 12–14 sampling steps and without acceleration LoRAs; each eight-second take requires roughly three to four minutes. Reject weak candidates early through a preview node adapted from an LTX workflow. Reprocess selected H3 takes in LTX 2.5 to reach 2K and stabilize artifacts, then use Natron bridge nodes and an in-house WebGL compositor for masks, clean plates and finishing.
- **Reusable point:** separate shot authority by need—looser base references for exploratory motion, start frames for exact composition—and make fast base-model retakes the selection stage before spending refinement and compositing time. Early previews turn generation into a shot-picking loop instead of a finish-every-take loop.
- **Model evidence:** the original post explicitly names “MiniMax H3 as the base model for video generation,” gives the 1K / 12–14-step settings, states that no acceleration LoRA was used, and separately identifies LTX 2.5 as the downstream refiner/upscaler. This is a hybrid finished film, not a claim that the final 2K frames are untouched H3 output.
