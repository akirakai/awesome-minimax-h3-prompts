- **Aspect ratio:** Not disclosed in the indexed post.
- **Duration:** 15 seconds.
- **H3 evidence:** The creator explicitly labels it “MiniMax H3 + Suno” and publishes a “MiniMax H3 Prompt” with the generated video.
- **Original prompt excerpt:** “Use @[audio ref] as the timing, rhythm and editing reference.”
- **Accurate workflow summary:** Use one character reference as the strict authority for identity, proportions, hairstyle, outfit, colors, and style. Assign the audio reference a separate job: it controls beat timing, rhythm, performance energy, and edit cadence. Keep the character unchanged while cuts, gestures, pose changes, and camera emphasis follow the music.
- **Reusable prompt template:**

  `Use @[character reference] as the strict character authority. Preserve the exact identity, proportions, hairstyle, outfit, colors, materials, and visual style throughout. Use @[audio reference] only as the timing, rhythm, performance, and editing reference. Create a 15-second showcase in which each pose change, gesture, camera emphasis, and cut lands on a clear musical beat. Keep the same single character across every shot; vary framing and performance energy without redesigning the subject.`

- **Reusable pattern:** Separate *what must stay fixed* from *what is allowed to drive change*: the image owns identity and styling; the audio owns time, rhythm, performance, and cuts.
