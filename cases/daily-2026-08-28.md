- **Available output metadata:** approximately 15.17 seconds; 2560×1440; 16:9. Gadgetify’s independent reuse is also 15 seconds.
- **Model evidence:** the main post explicitly says “MiniMax H3,” contains the playable generated video, and links a direct reply headed “MiniMax H3 Prompt.” Gadgetify explicitly states that the same H3 formula was reused for another anime and attaches the result.
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Replace only {ANIME_TITLE}. Infer fifteen strong but spoiler-safe visual clues and order them from ambiguous to increasingly recognizable. Never show the title, character names, logos, answer cards, explanatory text, or major spoilers. Use an expressive mixed-2D language—anime linework, cel-shaded silhouettes, ink, painted shadows, screen-print and paper grain, halftone, graphic cutouts, editorial collage, symbols, speed lines, impact frames, smears, and brush transitions—while explicitly banning flat-vector explainer styling and 3D CGI. Derive palette, line quality, texture, typography, and transition language from the selected anime. Make every beat compositionally different, transform one clue into the next through shared shape, color, line, texture, or motion, accelerate the final beats by remixing prior motifs, and end abruptly on one unresolved clue.
- **Reusable pattern:** treat a title as the only variable and keep the reveal grammar fixed: spoiler-safe clue extraction, progressive recognizability, heterogeneous compositions, causal visual transformations, a dense recap climax, and no answer reveal. The independent reuse is useful evidence that the template transfers beyond one title.

- **Available output metadata:** approximately 15.20 seconds; prompt-specified 16:9.
- **Model evidence:** PixVerse states that the attached result tests MiniMax H3, says it was generated entirely from one text prompt, and publishes that complete prompt in a direct reply.
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Lock a Xuan-paper-white field, flat black silhouettes, mineral blue and restrained cinnabar red, paper grain, dry-brush edges, hard masks, layered depth, and causal motion. Allow only “THE LAST DRAW,” “UNDER THE RAIN,” and “NO ROAD BACK,” all uppercase, correctly spelled, legible, and treated as moving subjects rather than subtitles. Across five main timed sections plus a final triptych, make each title arise from its physical source: sword resonance writes the first, umbrella wedges and rain form the second, and a horse’s hoof arc draws the third. Every transient receives two to four anticipation frames, contact on the sound, and an immediate visual result. Synchronize type, camera, masks, and depth to close-miked stereo Foley; use AE-style shape layers, track mattes, dry-brush masks, staggered letters, parented rotation, reverse motion, impact frames, hard exits, and occlusion. End at 15.0 seconds in black and silence. Ban extra text, logos, misspellings, gradients, glossy 3D, photorealism, neon, HUDs, deformation, excessive particles, generic pans, slow zooms, dissolves, and unrelated motion.
- **Reusable pattern:** build a closed text ledger first, then give every word a visible sound-producing mechanism. A practical micro-timing law is anticipation → transient/contact → immediate type/camera/mask/depth response; this is more reusable than merely asking for “beat sync.”
