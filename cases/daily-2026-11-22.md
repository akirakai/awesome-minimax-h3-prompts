## 1. smereces — native Ref2VA changes a forest into active snowfall while preserving motion

- **Author:** u/smereces
- **Published:** 2026-11-21
- **Video / source / prompt:** [original Reddit post with embedded comparison video and full prompt](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1y1uy79/minimax_h3_native_edit_anything_perfect/)
- **Format / duration:** not disclosed in the accessible source
- **Theme:** replace a lush green forest and its weather with a snow-covered winter landscape and active snowfall while keeping the backpacked subject, clothing, performance, timing, camera, framing, perspective and soundtrack unchanged
- **Prompt, accurately summarized:**
  - Declare `<Video 1>` the sole authority for motion, performance, timing, camera movement, framing, perspective, subject positions, interactions and temporal structure.
  - In `retention_analysis`, assign explicit transfer modes: the original environment and weather are `fully_replaced`; lighting is `partially_modified` into cool diffuse snowy light; motion and camera are `fully_preserved`.
  - Replace the ground, trees/distant scenery and sky with snow-covered winter equivalents, then add frame-consistent snowfall with realistic flake size and speed.
  - Preserve the subject's identity, clothes, exact actions, poses, gestures, head movement, trajectory and sequence; do not morph, flicker, drift or re-stage the performance.
  - Preserve the original paired soundtrack, ambience, diegetic sounds, music and audiovisual sync unless audio is intentionally replaced.
- **Reusable point:** for reference-video edits, define a closed attribute contract before describing the look. Separate what must be replaced, adapted and preserved; make the source video authoritative for motion/camera/timing, and lock audio independently so a visual weather edit does not silently rewrite the soundtrack.
- **Model evidence:** the main post explicitly says it uses “native MiniMax H3.” In the same thread, the author clarifies that this means the standard/default MiniMax Ref2V workflow in ComfyUI and that the posted prompt is used without an additional custom workflow.
