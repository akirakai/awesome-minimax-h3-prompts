- **Model evidence:** the post explicitly attributes actor generation, video character-sheet creation, and later Ref2VA scene generation to MiniMax H3 and attaches a demonstration video.
- **Workflow, accurately transcribed and structured:** First prompt a mundane or representative 5–10-second T2VA scene and generate several candidate actors cheaply. For an approved candidate, extract still frames and create a `.char` package containing body type, face, and audio. When speed matters more than temporal evidence, use stills from the character-sheet render instead of the video reference.
- **Reusable pattern:** separate actor audition, portable identity packaging, high-quality turnaround capture, and scene production. Spend little on broad candidate search, raise steps only after identity approval, store face/body/voice evidence together, and choose still versus video reference according to whether fidelity or temporal context is more important.
