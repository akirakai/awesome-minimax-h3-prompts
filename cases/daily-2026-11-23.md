### Reusable prompting lessons

- **Give every reference one job.** Name `@Image1`, `@Audio1` and `@Video1`, then state exactly whether each controls identity, soundtrack or camera movement.
- **For motion transfer, close the transfer scope.** “Copy only the camera movement” plus “do not copy the landscape or colours” prevents the source scene from leaking into the new shot.
- **Match action count to duration.** The chef test did not clearly complete every requested toss/catch beat; one large action plus a verifiable ending is safer in eight seconds.
- **Design FL2VA as a visible path.** Keep start and end frames spatially aligned, lock the camera, then describe only the intermediate state changes.
- **Audit identity in every scale.** The perfume matched in close-up but drifted in the opening wide shot; product proportions must be checked shot by shot.
- **Treat generated speech as a deliverable to verify.** The fisherman performed the action, but the exact quiet line was not clearly audible.
