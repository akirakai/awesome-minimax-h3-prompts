## 1. One-take ten-pose anime fashion editorial

- **Video and original post:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2093697204384887023
- **Full prompt:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2093697484333658336
- **Published:** 2026-08-29 22:48 JST; prompt reply at 22:49 JST
- **Author:** Kōda (@aimikoda)
- **Theme:** a square anime fashion editorial that cycles one character through ten distinct poses inside a white cyclorama without cutting the physical camera path.
- **Available output metadata:** prompt-specified 15 seconds and 128 BPM; the playable X transcode is approximately 15.08 seconds at 320×320, a 1:1 frame.
- **Model evidence:** the main post explicitly says “MiniMax H3 Anime Fashion Editorial in Motion,” contains the playable video, and links a direct reply headed “MiniMax H3 Prompt.”
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Bind the supplied character reference to the exact face, hair, outfit, accessories, proportions, and anime style, discard its background, and forbid new accessories. Use a minimal white cyclorama with hard strobes, faint haze, a glossy floor, satin, and proof sheets. Direct one uninterrupted 0–15 second physical camera path through ten pose states: frontal medium close-up; three-quarter and downcast collar/hair gestures; strict profile and shoulder roll; waist-level robe movement and over-shoulder look; floor-level kneeling pose with a foreground foot; rising three-quarter portrait; inward breath followed by an upward stretch; rear three-quarter shoulder silhouette; then an eye/neckline graze that arcs into a dominant low-angle full-body finish. Accelerate between poses and brake hard on every lock, with a smooth 24–85 mm zoom feel, shutter clicks, breathing, hair swish, and fabric movement. Preserve identity, anatomy, hair, outfit, accessories, and style throughout.
- **Reusable pattern:** treat a pose list as a continuous movement score rather than a montage. Give each pose an entry path, a brief locked hold, and an exit acceleration; a repeated “accelerate → brake → lock” rhythm makes ten distinct editorial compositions readable without cuts.

## 2. Three-prompt flat-gouache anthology with a shared visual grammar

- **Published:** 2026-08-29 18:25–18:26 JST
- **Author:** Magnific (@magnific)
- **Theme:** three independent flat-gouache stories—a blue-skinned woman standing apart in a sea of umbrellas, a helmeted office worker falling through a red screen-filled void, and a climber discovering that every mountain peak is already flagged.
- **Available output metadata:** individual clips are approximately 30.03, 14.97, and 30.00 seconds; the playable anthology is approximately 75.1 seconds. Aspect ratio and source resolution were not disclosed.
- **Model evidence:** the main post explicitly says the three separate generations were made with MiniMax H3, contains the assembled playable video, and each direct reply publishes a full prompt beside its source clip.
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Prompt 1 uses a blue-skinned woman with a black bob, red lips, blue kimono coat, and umbrella. It moves from a high-angle sea of blue umbrellas on a red-pink street to an under-brim close-up, ground-level moving legs against her planted feet, an eye-open beat, a sky change from black to bright blue as rain stops, and a symmetric crosswalk ending where only her umbrella is closed. Prompt 2 follows pale hands typing, a symmetric colored-monitor grid, a red-room rupture where screens tear free and trail cables, a push into the worker’s featureless helmet, and a fall through a red void ending on hard black. Prompt 3 begins with hands gripping a night ridge, follows the woman up an icy peak, plants a red flag, pulls back to reveal every peak already flagged, and ends when a huge profile slides in from frame right. All three lock the same flat gouache/collage grammar, hard edges, paper grain, restricted cobalt/electric blue, hot pink/red, and black palettes, while directing scene-specific foley, music, and silence separately.
- **Reusable pattern:** create an anthology style ledger that survives independent generations: fix medium, edge quality, paper texture, palette, contrast, and sound philosophy globally, then give each clip its own simple object/action metaphor and final visual reversal. Consistency comes from the shared grammar, not from reusing one subject.

## 3. HR Endless Sampler: base-H3 long-video generation on 16 GB VRAM

- **Original post and playable video:** https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1w25d7g/hr_endless_sampler_now_you_can_create_minimax_h3/
- **Direct GitHub project:** https://github.com/hradec/ComfyUI-HR-Endless-Sampler
- **Example workflow:** https://github.com/hradec/ComfyUI-HR-Endless-Sampler/blob/main/example_workflows/HR-Endless-Sampler-Example.json
- **Example video:** https://github.com/hradec/ComfyUI-HR-Endless-Sampler/blob/main/example_workflows/videos/HR_Endless_Sampler_00001_.mp4
- **Generated per-chunk prompt timeline:** https://github.com/hradec/ComfyUI-HR-Endless-Sampler/blob/main/example_workflows/videos/HR_Endless_Sampler_00001_.mp4.hr_endless_sampler_timeline.json
- **Published:** 2026-08-30 11:36 JST
- **Author:** rhradec
- **Theme:** a roughly 26-second live-action fantasy parody in which two riders on a tiger enter an overgrown temple, stop and investigate, encounter a multi-headed dragon, and flee.
- **Available output metadata:** approximately 26 seconds at 1080p and 24 fps; the disclosed project uses 625 source frames and a 1920×1088 working frame. The example is documented as generated on a 16 GB GPU.
- **Model evidence:** the repository states that the node currently supports MiniMax H3, the downloadable workflow actively loads the official base `minimax_h3_ref2va_pruned_int8_convrot.safetensors`, MiniMax H3 video/audio VAEs, and the MiniMax Qwen text encoder. The optional Turbo and realism LoRA loader nodes in the graph are in bypass mode, so the example is attributable to base H3 rather than a derivative-only acceleration output.
- **Accurate prompt/workflow summary:** The source project defines four referenced subjects and one temple reference, then supplies a seven-shot, 625-frame story ledger: jungle side-track and arc toward the pyramid; fast entry and a deliberately cartoony tiger skid; a close-up warning line; dismount and walk; a shaking-room close-up with two lines; a frightened reaction and dragon roar; then a wide low-angle gate collapse, multi-headed dragon reveal, panic line, and retreat. The sampler divides this timeline into 39-frame chunks, carries 22 continuation frames from the previous result, and uses Gemma 4 12B QAT as a chunk director. Gemma sees prior output at 2 fps plus the exact final frame, rewrites only the current local timecodes into H3 `[Shot N]` syntax, and records every final per-chunk `detailed_description` in a sidecar timeline. The author explicitly documents one failure where Gemma repeated dialogue after assigning one shot description to the next chunk, making the error trace reproducible.
- **Reusable pattern:** separate a long-form source timeline from short generation windows. Preserve a small temporal overlap and the exact boundary frame, ask a vision-language director to inspect the accepted output before rewriting only the next local chunk, and save those rewritten prompts beside the video. For 16 GB at 1080p, the project recommends 39-frame chunks without special low-VRAM attention or about 56 frames with its more aggressive low-VRAM setting; 22 continuation frames are the documented default.
