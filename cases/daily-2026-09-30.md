Never show the hunter device in a clean exterior shot: establish it only through a red core reflected first in the drink glass, then in the window, synchronized with the cat harness warning and fine glass vibration. Keep the emotional line teasing control, soft attention, arrested surprise, alert recognition and protective danger response.

Five timecoded beats use a low side-follow, gentle half-orbit, short push-pull, static rack-focus danger beat, lateral dive with one shock wobble, then a tiny settling rebound. She sets down the drink and reaches for the cat; the cat pins her wrist and looks toward the reflection. Only after she follows that eyeline may she scoop the cat up, lower her center of gravity and dive behind the right side of the bar. The window must crack, fail and burst in that order, half a beat after they leave the exposed spot. Track uneven shards, amber liquid viscosity and fan splash, dust, sparks, delayed hat/hair/strap/fur motion, locked breath and restarted breathing. End in cover with an unresolved threat, settling debris and three quiet English lines; no exterior cut, heroic swell, crowd or black ending.

### Reusable pattern

Make protection a physically completed move before impact, then stage the failure as crack, structural release and material aftermath so the near miss remains causally readable.
