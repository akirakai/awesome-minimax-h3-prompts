## 1. Vintage Binocular Brand Film

- **Endpoint:** Reference-to-video
- **Prompt focus:** Sequential keyframes, fixed binocular mask, whip transitions, rack focus, motion blur, optical smearing, exposure flicker, and restrained 35mm film texture.
- **Example / prompt:** [Vintage Binocular Brand Film on the guide](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/minimax-h3-prompting-guide)

### Accurate prompt summary

Use Images 1–4 as sequential keyframes inside a vintage binocular viewfinder searching for a MiniMax installation. Begin out of focus with subtle handheld shake, push quickly toward Image 1, and use fast scan transitions between keyframes. Cut at peak blur, then settle and return to focus.

Keep the twin circular lens mask fixed in position, scale, feathering, and edge softness. Only the content inside the mask may move. Preserve the installation and core compositions. Let fabric, people, and typography move naturally without spins, bounces, or oversized fly-ins. Finish with fine grain, soft halation, restrained colors, and red typographic accents.

### Reusable pattern

When animating several reference images, define:

- the order of the references,
- the part of the frame that is allowed to move,
- the transition vocabulary,
- the fixed mask or layout constraints,
- how typography enters and exits.

## 2. Snowy Bamboo Wuxia Mystery

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Endpoint:** Reference-to-video
- **Prompt focus:** Photoreal period drama, cold low-saturation palette, snow, mist, shallow depth of field, close-ups, and shot/reverse-shot coverage.
- **Example / prompt:** [Snowy Bamboo Wuxia Mystery on the guide](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/minimax-h3-prompting-guide)

### Accurate prompt summary

Create a 16:9 cinematic wuxia mystery at night in a bamboo forest. Use cold blue, ink green, charcoal, and gray with thin mist and fine snow. Keep dense vertical bamboo in the background, soft foreground leaves, cool side key light, dark foreground, luminous distance, and shallow depth of field.

Prioritize facial close-ups and restrained shot/reverse-shot coverage. Maintain photoreal period-drama production value. Exclude modern elements, subtitles, watermarks, animation styling, over-smoothed skin, bright daylight, and comic performances.

### Reusable pattern

Use visual constraints to prevent the model from drifting into generic fantasy:

- palette,
- period,
- lighting direction,
- depth-of-field behavior,
- shot coverage,
- explicit exclusions.

## 3. Futuristic Eyewear Campaign

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16-oriented fashion campaign example
- **Endpoint:** Reference-to-video
- **Prompt focus:** Reference-role assignment, fashion direction, product consistency, and motion/edit rhythm.
- **Example / prompt:** [Futuristic Eyewear Campaign on the guide](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/minimax-h3-prompting-guide)

### Accurate prompt summary

Create a premium vertical fashion-eyewear commercial in a seamless white studio. Match the reference video's shot rhythm, edit speed, cyclorama look, and severe fashion attitude. Use separate references for the key visual and two models, facial details, and the eyewear design. Preserve the models' wardrobe, body language, lighting, runway presence, and cool attitude while making the eyewear read as a premium futuristic accessory.

### Reusable pattern

For product or fashion work, assign references by function rather than saying “use these images”:

- overall composition and lighting,
- talent identity and body language,
- facial details,
- product shape and material,
- edit rhythm.

## General techniques from the guide

- Use Text-to-Video when there are no media references.
- Use First & Last Frame when the uploaded images are literal endpoints.
- Use Reference-to-Video when images, video, or audio are references rather than endpoint frames.
- H3 supports up to 9 images, 3 video clips, and 3 audio clips in the guide's reference workflow.
- For multi-beat clips, write a timecoded shot list and direct audio as deliberately as the picture.
