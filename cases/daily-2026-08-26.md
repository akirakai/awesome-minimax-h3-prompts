## 1. One-shot miniature city-map assembly

- **Video and original post:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2092245028399726605
- **Full prompt:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2092245330464997846
- **Published:** 2026-08-25 22:38 JST
- **Author:** Kōda (@aimikoda)
- **Theme:** a stylized 3D miniature city map assembles itself along a fast continuous camera move, ending on a culturally adapted city-name title.
- **Available output metadata:** 15 seconds; aspect ratio and resolution were not disclosed.
- **Model evidence:** both the video post and prompt reply explicitly identify MiniMax H3; the video is attached to the original post.
- **Accurate prompt summary:** Replace a single `[CITY]` variable. Build a compact solid-surface diorama from the city’s most recognizable streets, parks, bridges, waterfronts, terrain, and landmarks. Move quickly through it in one continuous shot, slowing briefly at major locations. Buildings and supporting elements should rise along the camera path with overshoot, bounce, and clean settling, so the city progressively “awakens through memory.” Do not show the city name during the map sequence; reserve it for the final pullback and design the title typography around the city’s local visual identity.
- **Reusable pattern:** use one editable location variable, a fixed assembly grammar, and a delayed title reveal. Alternating fast travel with brief landmark pauses makes a dense 15-second geography readable.

- **Accurate prompt summary:** Lock the heroine’s exact face and anime proportions but require each identity to have a different hair silhouette, color, costume, makeup, accessories, and attitude. Use object-driven transition bridges instead of asking the character to morph directly.

## 7. Locally rendered 3.5-minute lip-sync music video

- **Video and original post:** https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1vylo9l/lipsync_music_video_minimax_h3_workflow/
- **Published workflow:** https://pastebin.com/05v805uj
- **Published:** 2026-08-26 12:33 JST
- **Author:** TheDerminator1337
- **Theme:** a full music video assembled from identity-consistent H3 lip-sync clips driven by a Suno song.
- **Available output metadata:** 14 clips × 15 seconds, approximately 210 seconds total; FL2VA + Ref2VA LoRA at about 1.4 MP, 20 steps; 3–4 hours per render cycle on RTX 5090 as reported by the author.
- **Model evidence:** the post explicitly names MiniMax H3, embeds the completed video, shares the workflow, and identifies the local FL2VA/Ref2VA configuration.
- **Accurate workflow summary:** Split the song into fourteen separately promptable 15-second clips and stitch them after generation. The short-segment approach prevents temporal degradation, but clothing can drift unless clothing references are supplied to each clip. The author recommends reducing future segments to about seven seconds for higher resolution and faster iteration. A lighter alternative uses approximately 1 MP, 8 steps, Turbo LoRA, sparse attention, and a 4B Qwen text encoder, reducing a clip to roughly 20–30 minutes on the same class of GPU.
- **Reusable pattern:** long-form lip sync is an assembly problem: segment the audio, keep each clip independently regenerable, reuse explicit identity/clothing references, and trade segment length against resolution, render time, and drift.
