## 1. arthan1011 — native Ref2VA character replacement without a swap LoRA

- **Author:** arthan1011
- **Published:** 2026-09-29; the Reddit page showed “3 hr. ago” during this run
- **Video, original post and full prompt:** [“You don't need character swap LoRA for MiniMax H3”](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1wsknup/you_dont_need_character_swap_lora_for_minimax_h3/)
- **Observed media:** split-screen comparison. Duration, resolution and aspect ratio were not disclosed.
- **Theme:** replace the performer in an anime motion reference while retaining a rapid orbiting camera and keeping the replacement character's face front-facing.
- **Model evidence:** the title and body explicitly identify MiniMax H3 Ref2VA. The comparison labels the right-hand clip “No LoRA,” and the author states that both clips use the same prompt; only that right-hand base-H3 result is included here.

### Reusable pattern

For native Ref2VA character replacement, assign identity to one video and one still, camera motion and framing to a separate video, and environment to another still. State preservation versus attribute-transfer roles explicitly, require the replacement face to remain front-facing through the orbit, and use a same-prompt no-LoRA comparison to verify that the base model performs the swap.
