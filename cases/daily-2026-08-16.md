## 1. Reference-still reverse engineering for reusable scene prompts

- **Published:** 2026-08-15 23:43 JST
- **Author:** Kōda (`@aimikoda`)
- **Source, showcase videos, and workflow:** [X post](https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2088637730171363633)
- **Theme:** Rebuild the scene grammar, composition, and mood of an existing still while adapting it into a new H3 video concept
- **Duration / ratio:** Multiple showcase clips; exact settings were not disclosed in the indexed post
- **H3 evidence:** The creator explicitly labels the showcased outputs MiniMax H3 and explains how the prompts used for those videos were produced.

### Published workflow — accurate summary

1. Find a still image whose **scene, composition, and mood** match the target. The creator used YouTube playlist thumbnails and recommends Pinterest as another source.
2. Give the still to a vision-capable LLM and ask for a detailed, generation-oriented description rather than a loose caption.
3. Use that visual analysis as the scene brief for H3, then replace source-specific identity details with the intended character and desired action.
4. Keep spatial layout, lighting relationships, color palette, and atmosphere from the inspiration while avoiding literal copying of incidental subjects or text.
5. Before generation, rewrite the description as temporal direction: opening state, visible motion, camera behavior, and final state.

### Reusable template

```text
Use the supplied inspiration still only as a scene-grammar reference.

Preserve:
- composition and subject placement
- camera height, lens feeling, and depth layering
- lighting direction, palette, weather, and atmosphere

Replace:
- the original person/object with [TARGET SUBJECT]
- the original activity with [VISIBLE ACTION]
- incidental signage or text with [NONE / SPECIFIED TEXT]

Animate the scene as:
[OPENING STATE] → [PHYSICAL CHANGE] → [ENDING STATE]

Camera:
[CAMERA MOVE, RANGE, SPEED]

Sound:
[AMBIENCE + SYNCHRONIZED FOLEY]
Music:
[MUSIC DIRECTION / N/A]
```

### Reusable pattern

Treat a found image as a **visual grammar reference**, not as a prompt to copy everything visible. Separating locked composition and mood from replaceable identity, action, and text makes the method reusable across characters and genres.

## 2. Fifteen-second farmers-market micro-vlog with timed dialogue

- **Published:** 2026-08-16 12:57 JST
- **Author:** M (`@Strength04_X`)
- **Video / H3 identification:** [X video post](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2088837429046477102)
- **Prompt reply:** [X prompt](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2088837475670376713)
- **Theme:** A casually dressed young woman shops for peaches and sunflowers at a market while speaking naturally to camera
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Aspect ratio / resolution:** Not disclosed
- **H3 evidence:** The video post explicitly states that it was made with MiniMax H3 on Hailuo AI; the immediately linked reply publishes the subject description and full five-beat timeline.

### Published prompt structure — accurate summary

- Lock a young American woman in her early twenties: oversized flannel over a graphic T-shirt, light-wash jeans, white sneakers, loose beach-wave hair, and an everyday non-modelled appearance.
- **00:00–00:03:** She lifts a ripe peach, smells it, and smiles.
- **00:03–00:06:** She faces the camera, laughs softly, and says the peaches smell amazing.
- **00:06–00:09:** She pays the vendor and places the peach in her tote bag.
- **00:09–00:12:** She moves to the next stall and admires a bundle of sunflowers.
- **00:12–00:15:** She looks back to camera and begins saying she will buy them.
- Keep the performance conversational and lightly compressed: every three-second block contains two readable beats, but all actions remain physically ordinary.

### Reusable template

```text
Main subject:
[AGE / APPEARANCE], [OUTFIT], [HAIR], natural everyday performance.

Setting:
[REAL-WORLD LOCATION], available light, lived-in background activity.

Timeline (15 seconds; two compact beats per 3-second block):
00:00–00:03 — [OBJECT INTERACTION], then [REACTION].
00:03–00:06 — Turn to camera and say [SHORT NATURAL LINE].
00:06–00:09 — [TRANSACTION / PROP HANDOFF], then secure the prop.
00:09–00:12 — Move to a nearby point of interest and inspect [SECOND OBJECT].
00:12–00:15 — Return attention to camera and deliver [PAYOFF LINE].

Audio:
Natural location ambience, close dialogue, subtle object and clothing foley; no exaggerated performance.
```

### Reusable pattern

For a convincing 15-second lifestyle clip, organize the prompt into **five three-second blocks with two compact actions each**. Alternate object interaction, camera-facing dialogue, a concrete transaction, movement to a second location, and a closing line; this creates a complete micro-story without cinematic over-direction.
