- **Author:** Ima Studio (@ImaStudio_ai)

- **Observed media:** 15.697 seconds, 3840×2160, 16:9; prompt target 15 seconds at 24 fps.
- **Theme:** one adult Korean model progresses through six rose-pink luxury-editorial looks and framings, ending on a half-second frozen cover frame.
- **Model evidence:** the original post says “Made with MiniMax H3 in Ima Studio,” links the H3 product page, and publishes the entire case prompt beside the playable clip.

### Accurate prompt summary

The storyboard owns shot order and composition, but every panel must become a full-screen shot rather than a grid. One exact woman, face, hair, skin tone and body proportion persist through all six 2.5-second sections; only outfit and background may change. The camera stays in shoulder-up or chest-up beauty range and uses slow push-ins, subtle lateral slides, micro-dolly motion and translucent foreground wipes. Acting is limited to eye movement, gentle head turns, hair touches, small shoulder movement and a slight chin lift. Text is explicitly reserved for post-production instead of being rendered by H3.

### Reusable pattern

Assign the storyboard authority over shot order and framing, the reference identity authority over the performer, and the text prompt authority over motion, materials and transitions. Protect identity with a closed consistency list, keep movement small enough for beauty close-ups, and reserve the last half-second as a truly static cover frame. Add typography later instead of making it compete with face consistency.
