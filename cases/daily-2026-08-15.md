## 1. Ref2VA motion transfer: replace a walking person with a velociraptor

- **Published:** 2026-08-15 08:53 JST
- **Author:** Hoje-Na-IA (`u/Hoje-Na-IA`)
- **Source and prompt thread:** [Reddit / r-StableDiffusion](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1vonhld/testing_v2v_on_minimax_h3/)
- **Direct video:** [13-second Reddit video](https://v.redd.it/etqartojhfjh1)
- **Theme:** Replace a man walking through a home interior with a reference-image velociraptor while retaining the source video's motion and scene
- **Duration / ratio:** 13 seconds; 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **Inputs:** One source video plus one velociraptor image
- **H3 evidence:** The creator explicitly identifies the workflow as “Minimax H3 ref2va,” names the two inputs, embeds the result, and publishes both the generated prompt and the short instruction used to produce it with the official H3 reference-prompt guide.

### Published prompt structure — accurate summary

- Define the room, furniture, lighting, and atmosphere from `<Video 1>` as one persistent subject.
- Define the velociraptor from `<Picture 1>` as a second subject whose anatomy, scale texture, and color pattern must remain stable.
- Treat the source video as the authority for the background, camera motion, temporal pacing, and walking path.
- Replace only the man: the raptor follows the same trajectory, speed, step timing, camera distance, and camera angle.
- Re-light the raptor and update its floor shadow to match the room instead of pasting it over the footage.
- Preserve the original room tone, replace human footsteps with reptilian claw clicks, and add no music.

The creator generated this structured H3 prompt by asking a local LLM to write a human-to-raptor replacement prompt and supplying MiniMax's official Ref2VA prompt-writing guide.

### Reusable pattern

For performance transfer, let the source video own **trajectory, timing, camera, and environment**, while the image owns **replacement identity and surface detail**. Explicitly replacing contact sounds and re-lighting the new subject helps the edit feel physically integrated.

## 2. Two-reference park narrative with a timed character handoff

- **Published:** 2026-08-15 01:07 JST
- **Author:** cardoso (`@Cardoso`)
- **Source, video, and prompt:** [X post](https://x.com/Cardoso/status/2088296385422455165)
- **Theme:** A woman walks through a summer park, then a hard cut introduces a second referenced character seated on a bench
- **Resolution:** 720p
- **Duration / ratio:** Not fully disclosed in the indexed post
- **Inputs:** Two reference images
- **H3 evidence:** The creator states the video was rendered with MiniMax H3 using two reference images and publishes the generated video and shot prompt in the same post.

### Published prompt structure — accurate summary

- Use `<Picture 1>` as the exact opening frame.
- Open on a bright, tree-lined park and lock the woman's distinctive outfit, hat, boots, and accessories to the first reference.
- Follow her with a wide tracking shot as she walks.
- At exactly 00:04, hard-cut to a wooden bench beside the path.
- Introduce the man from `<Picture 2>` seated and absorbed in a modern smartphone, preserving his dark clothing and reference identity.

### Reusable pattern

When two references enter at different times, bind each person to a named picture and make the change of subject a **timed editorial event**. An exact first-frame anchor stabilizes the opening character; a hard cut creates a clean boundary for introducing the second identity.

## 3. Local T2VA pool scene with timed Japanese dialogue and sound layers

- **Published:** 2026-08-13 06:04 JST
- **Author:** Kazuhisa Nishikawa / PC Watch
- **Source, embedded video, and prompt:** [PC Watch MiniMax H3 hands-on](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/column/nishikawa/2132431.html)
- **Theme:** A young woman talks to camera at an outdoor pool, jumps in, and reacts to the cold water
- **Format:** Local MiniMax H3 T2V workflow in ComfyUI; Turbo LoRA 0.8 / 8 steps plus attention optimizations
- **Duration / resolution:** 10 seconds; 720p; 24 fps
- **Aspect ratio:** Not disclosed
- **H3 evidence:** The author identifies the exact ComfyUI MiniMax H3 T2V workflow, publishes the timed prompt, embeds the result, and reports the local render settings and duration.

### Published prompt structure — accurate summary

- **00:00–00:03:** Medium shot of a young Japanese woman in a white sundress sitting at a sunny pool with her feet in the water; she cheerfully says in Japanese that she has come to the pool for the first time in a while.
- **00:03–00:05:** Cut to a close-up as she stands, smiles, and shouts a short Japanese exclamation before jumping.
- **00:05–00:10:** Cut wide as she dives, splashes, resurfaces laughing, and says the water is cold.
- Put every spoken line inside H3's language-tagged dialogue markup.
- Separate diegetic ambience—water splashes and summer birds—from a light, relaxed ukulele cue that fades at the end.

### Reusable pattern

A compact T2VA prompt can coordinate **shot timing, framing, physical action, multilingual dialogue, ambience, and music** in one generation. Keep dialogue inside each shot block, then specify environmental sound and non-diegetic music as separate layers.
