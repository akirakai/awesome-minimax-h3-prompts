The page presents copy-ready MiniMax H3 shot briefs with example clips. The briefs consistently combine timed beats, visual constraints, sound direction, and negative constraints.

## 1. Rooftop Glasshouse Fragrance Film

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Prompt:**

  A fragrance film inside a rooftop glasshouse at dusk, with city haze pressing against the panes. From 0–5 seconds, a woman moves between benches of orchids and mists the leaves. From 5–10 seconds, she pauses and lifts a small faceted bottle so the last light passes through it. From 10–15 seconds, she sets the bottle among the flowers and the camera drifts away through the glass. Keep the air humid, show condensation, use restrained color, and reserve one amber accent. Sound: faint irrigation drip, distant traffic, and one sustained cello note. No dialogue and no on-screen text.

## 2. Sodium Lamp Running Film

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Prompt:**

  A running film on an empty wet street at night, lit almost entirely by one orange sodium lamp. From 0–5 seconds, a runner enters from darkness and passes through the light. From 5–10 seconds, use slow motion on the foot strike and spray from the road. From 10–15 seconds, the runner exits into darkness while only the sound continues. Let the shoe read through silhouette, wet reflections, and one clean profile frame rather than a conventional beauty shot. Sound: breath, footfall on wet asphalt, and a low sub pulse on each strike. No music drop, logos, or text.

- **Source / example clip:** [Morphic prompt library](https://morphic.com/resources/how-to/minimax-h3-prompts)

## 3. Tonearm Needle Drop

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Prompt:**

  A product film of a turntable in a dim room, with one hard raking light from frame left revealing the machined edges. From 0–5 seconds, slowly orbit the platter as the record reaches speed and the label blurs into a solid ring. From 5–10 seconds, cut to an extreme macro as the tonearm swings over and the stylus settles into the lead-in groove, with dust motes crossing the beam. From 10–15 seconds, pull back as the arm tracks inward. Use brushed aluminum, a matte-black plinth, and one warm amber indicator. Sound: mechanical settling, stylus drop, and surface crackle before any music. No hands, branding, or text.

- **Source / example clip:** [Morphic prompt library](https://morphic.com/resources/how-to/minimax-h3-prompts)

## Reusable pattern

The briefs follow a compact five-part structure:

1. Roles and subject.
2. Timecoded beats.
3. Look and camera behavior.
4. Sound and dialogue.
5. Limits and exclusions.
