### 3. Seamless long-video continuation from overlapping 10-second sections

- **Author:** `rcscs`
- **Published:** 2026-08-09
- **Original post:** https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1vj3zi3/a_technique_for_creating_seamless_continuous/
- **Theme:** Extend MiniMax H3 videos in 10-second sections without restarting motion or losing subject identity.
- **Duration / timing:** Generate 10-second sections; feed the final 2 seconds (48 frames at 24 fps) of the previous section as `<Video 1>`.
- **H3 evidence:** The post title and body explicitly identify MiniMax H3 and describe the creator’s tested continuation workflow.
- **Published prompt wording:**

  `[Shot 1]`

  `Target video is a seamless continuation of <Video 1>. First frame of [Shot 1] is the last frame of <Video 1>.`

- **Workflow:** Trim the previous clip to its final two seconds, connect that clip to the H3 reference-video input, reuse the same subject reference images on every generation, prepend the continuation wording above, then append the next section’s action.
- **Reusable pattern:** Give H3 both temporal overlap and an explicit frame-boundary statement. Reusing the identity references on every section reduces facial drift; the overlap carries motion direction and velocity into the next section.
