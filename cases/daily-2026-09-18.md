## 1. The performer’s hand acts as a physical camera controller

- **Video and original post:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2100645075931865482
- **Full prompt:** https://x.com/aimikoda/status/2100645325870420350
- **Published:** 2026-09-18 02:56 JST; prompt reply at 02:57 JST
- **Author:** Kōda (@aimikoda)
- **Theme:** an unarmed character controls a continuous camera path with the right hand, moving the lens from eye level to ankle height, overhead orbit, full-body retreat, and a final face close-up.
- **Available output metadata:** 15.083 seconds, 640 × 360 public stream, 16:9.
- **Model evidence:** the original post explicitly says “MiniMax H3 Camera control with Makima,” embeds the result, and links a reply beginning “MiniMax H3 Prompt.”
- **Original prompt, accurately condensed:** Use the character reference only for identity, anatomy, clothing, and visual style in an empty charcoal studio. One uninterrupted take; the right hand leads every camera movement and the lens must match its direction, timing, acceleration, braking, and final stop while the eyes continue following the lens. Begin in a frontal eye-level medium view: an inviting palm pulls the camera inward; a sharp downward sweep drives a rapid descent to ankle height; a rising hand lifts the camera through a pitched arc until it is above the hand looking vertically down; one clockwise hand circle produces one matching overhead orbit at fixed clearance; a lowered palm leads a descending arc back to eye level; a forward press drives a fast retreat to full-body view; and a hand drawn back to the cheek pulls the camera into a tight face close-up. Forbid cuts, hidden edits, lens-cover transitions, teleporting, digital zoom, continuing camera motion after the gesture stops, impact sounds, dialogue, text, and extra characters.
- **Reusable pattern:** define a visible body part as the camera’s motion source, make direction, speed, acceleration, and braking inherit from that source, and require the camera to stop on the same beat as the gesture.

## 2. The environment reacts to an unseen threat before the performer does

- **Video and original post:** https://x.com/ou_zhen599/status/2100849010278039850
- **Full prompt:** https://x.com/ou_zhen599/status/2100849938297184746
- **Published:** 2026-09-18 16:26 JST; prompt reply at 16:30 JST
- **Author:** Loriel.AI (@ou_zhen599)
- **Theme:** a returning explorer crosses one wet rainforest-temple canyon, says “我又回来了,” then hears two off-screen monster cries and moves into cover without the creature ever appearing.
- **Available output metadata:** 15.147 seconds, 640 × 360 public stream, 16:9.
- **Model evidence:** the original post explicitly states “Made with MiniMax-H3 ComfyUI Image-to-Video”; the reply is headed “MiniMax-H3 prompt” and publishes the complete timecoded prompt.
- **Original prompt, accurately condensed:** Preserve one connected canyon location and one explorer with unchanged face, clothing, gear, wet hair, mud, sweat, and proportions. Keep her moving through wet stone, puddles, rope, and one step-up while expressions progress from restrained familiarity to alertness. At 0–3 seconds, track from a broken pillar as she walks right-front, balances against stone, and makes visible ripples; at 3–5 seconds, stay close beside continuing motion and contact; at 5–8 seconds, rise with her step toward the ruins as she quietly says “我又回来了”; at 8–11 seconds, show real boot load, water flick, cloth folds, strap rebound, and a deeper breath. At 11–13 seconds, the first distant cry must disturb waterfall mist, vines, droplets, dust, and shallow water before her eyes and mouth change. At 13–15 seconds, a closer second cry triggers the hard stop, half-turn, one backward step behind a pillar, lower center of gravity, delayed hair and gear swing, splash, grit displacement, and a guarded final pose. The creature remains unseen; waterfall, dripping stone, boots, breath, leather, cloth, and the two spatially distinct cries stay dominant over only a minimal tension bed.
- **Reusable pattern:** when an off-screen cause must feel real, stage measurable environmental evidence first, let the camera hesitate long enough to register it, and only then allow the performer’s facial and defensive response.
