### Prompt, accurately summarized

Use the input image as the exact first frame and sole visual authority for the woman, motorcycle, street, outfit and daylight. Lock the same vintage motorcycle, wheel orientation, side stand, street geometry, sun direction and color palette. The engine remains off and both wheels remain motionless.

Start from the seated pose. She dismounts on the near side, walks along the fuel tank toward the front wheel, crouches, tests the tire tread, looks through the spokes, follows the fork upward, rises while inspecting the headlight, pushes her sunglasses onto her head, then crosses around the front of the headlight to the far side and finishes by pressing the seat cushion. The object she touches must always be the object she is looking at. During the side change, let the round headlight fill the foreground as a natural wipe so the route is visibly continuous rather than a teleport.

Divide the action into six timed phases: a fast weighted dismount and walk; slow tank-badge inspection; a sudden stable crouch; slow tire, hub and fork examination; a supported rise with one synchronized headlight tap; then one brief whip-pan around the lamp before the slow seat inspection. Her mouth, eyes, brow, breathing and shoulder tension gradually relax across the sequence. At about 13.6 seconds she says only, “Hi, gorgeous,” and the final frame keeps her hand on the seat.

Contact sounds must come from their visible sources: sandals on stone, grit cracking, fingertip on paint, tire rebound, knuckle on metal and seat compression. Hair, fringe and clothing lag only after body acceleration. No extra people, traffic, location change, motorcycle movement, mismatched touching and looking, body inserts, subtitles or voice-over.

### Reusable pattern

For a product-admiration scene, define a one-way inspection route and a closed checklist of product parts. Couple gaze, touch and camera framing at every checkpoint; prove every side change with a foreground landmark; and let emotional permission increase only after each physical test is completed.
